    Data. GroupLens MovieLens 32M (positive = rating ≥ 4). Canadian tags: Wikidata country of origin (P495), joined on IMDb ID. Phase 1 lines: GDELT GKG 2.0, World Bank, Microsoft MIND.
    Method. Item–item cosine with 40 neighbours; viewers with ≥ 15 liked films; last 5 held out; 10,952 eligible films. Settings re-rank the same scores. The team setting was picked on a separate 12,000 validation viewers with a rule fixed in advance: as little top-1% share as possible without losing accuracy.
    Limits. Top-10 lists are simulated from rating logs, not shown to people. MovieLens viewers are not a Canadian audience. Wikidata tags are a proxy, not CAVCO certification. No age, gender, race or politics is in these data or inferred.
